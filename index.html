ABOUT US

Who We Are

InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation was founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla. It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization based in Chhattisgarh.

The foundation works towards positive social change through community-driven initiatives across areas such as education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and skill development.

It believes in collective action and encourages volunteers, professionals and supporters to contribute towards meaningful causes.

OUR PROJECTS

Our Key Initiatives

Six initiatives focused on creating positive social and environmental change.

Project Seva

Providing food and clothing to underprivileged people and supporting communities in need.

Project Bachpanshala

Working to provide quality education and learning opportunities for underprivileged children.

Project Jeev

Supporting animal welfare through rescue, protection, feeding and care for animals.

Project Udaan

Empowering women through skill development and opportunities for financial independence.

Project Prakriti

Promoting environmental conservation, sustainability and awareness for a greener future.

Project Vikas

Enhancing employability through skill development and opportunities for growth.
